# Problem

    People who spend lots of time on the computer eventually want to know fast they can type. Maybe some people want to imporve their typing speed. Or maybe some people might want to see how fast they type compared to others.

# Initial requriements

    My program will adress this problem by:

        -   providing users with a working typing test
        -   returning a score measured in words per minute (wpm)
        -   returning a percentage indicating the percentage of people who got score lower than them
